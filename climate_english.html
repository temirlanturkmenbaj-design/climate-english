Climate Change and Our Future

Climate change is one of the major environmental challenges of our time. The Earth's average temperature is increasing, and this process is known as global warming. Human activities such as burning fossil fuels produce greenhouse gases, including carbon dioxide.

Climate change can lead to extreme weather, longer heatwaves, severe droughts, and more frequent floods. It can also contribute to melting glaciers and rising sea levels.

There are solutions. Renewable energy, such as solar and wind energy, can reduce carbon emissions. People can also save energy, recycle waste, protect forests and reduce their carbon footprint.

Find these words: climate change · global warming · fossil fuels · greenhouse gases · drought · renewable energy · carbon footprint
